## Part I: The Centaur's Nervous System

Similar to my previous essays, martial arts provides an intuition pump for what I want to express. Imagine learning how to wield a sword. When you start, there is a sword, and there is you. They are separate and you feel them as separate. At this stage you are nearly as likely to injure yourself as you are to strike your target. Now imagine training with this instrument for years. You start to know the position of the sword without having to see it. You can feel through the sword and intimately know the force a particular strike will impart. Are you and the sword separate? You are separable, but with training that instrument becomes an extension of your self-hood in a manner totally different from the experience the beginner faces. Both your internal world as well as your physical musculature and nervous system have adapted and adopted this extension. You are materially different compared to who you were before you started training.

One step further. Imagine you are a knight in training. A knight is dangerous not so much
because of their sword, but because they are a mounted warrior. The horse and the man unite to
become a much more dangerous weapon than either entity on their own. But once again we are faced
with the same progression. When you begin, your steed is separate from yourself. You are
just as likely to injure yourself at this stage as to move and operate according to your intentions.
You must train a long time before horse and identity are united.

The master horseback rider and the master swordsman both have changed their physical and mental identities. But the means and matter of their respective transformations are very different. For a horseback rider to be effective, both the horse and the rider must intimately know each other and trust one another. For the swordsman to be effective they must know the nature of their blade. Whatever possible awareness within the blade is moot. The action of the wielder solely determines the blade's position. Whereas the horse and rider have an ongoing negotiation on who is in charge, whose will leads and whose follows in the direction or purpose of action.

What distinguishes both these masters from beginners is how their felt sense extends through and beyond their body into their instrument. They have subconscious and pre-analytical feedback from the sword, from the horse. Relative to their motives, they can feel what movement is "right" or "wrong".

The OODA loop, coined by Colonel John Boyd points out how some entities, like humans and horses, observe, orient, decide, and act (OODA) in a dynamic loop. Some entities, like swords and hammers, do not. But critically, living beings are composed of a countless number of such loops. Each cell has an OODA loop, each organ, perhaps each brain hemisphere. Yet within that cacophony is you. Multiple OODA loops do not define what is or isn't you, instead how they interact---who is "inside" the others loop determines where the locus of action lives.

But inside doesn't mean what you think it means. A cell is inside me, but my cellular OODA loops are not 'inside' my phenomenology. What determines who is inside versus who is outside isn't a question of frequency or material size. What is inside vs outside is a question of whose principal is most respected. Whose virtues are the normative factor that determines what is observed, where it's oriented, what is decided, what is enacted. It is entirely feasible for a smaller, slower entity to be inside the other's OODA loop. When one entity can feel into the others loop, correctly anticipating when why and how their OODA will function, they can extend Tai Chi's effortless effort---the directing of external force according to your principles.

This is what determines the difference between a beginning rider and experienced cavalry. Going into
battle is fearful. A skillful rider must accept that fear and acknowledge it. They must predict its
limits and console the fear that two warm-blooded animals face in an existential situation. Whether
you charge or balk is a question of who you are. Are you the horse? Yes. Are you the rider? Yes.
When you accept you are both and neither then you can orient both towards what you value instead of
what scares you.

What does the master drone operator embody? Do the autonomous swarms form an extension of her will, or is her unique agency diluted and buffeted without firm control of whose OODA loop is inside whose. Is it the company that made the AI targeting software inside our loop or are we inside theirs? What role do the chips and hardware which constitute each drone play? Is it the general whose command told the operator to send the attack force? Perhaps the drones themselves are in control? The answer to this question is an OODA loop question. Who is most inside who? And the answer to that question is best couched as one of proprioception. Whoever has a firmer 'felt sense' of the other can use that sense to effectively extend effortless effort to direct their energy. When no single entity purposefully integrates the others they are incorporated with, a moloch appears. Such demons show themselves when control is so diluted that the body has no functioning head, no entity to claim responsibility for the principles enacted.

Warfare points to another important fact related to multi OODA loop mastery. Control can have loci, but where such loci are situated is scale dependent. This is what separates effective organizations (and organisms) from ineffective ones. An effective commander is "within" their unit's OODA loop in a manner that is effectively different than how each soldier internalizes their commander. In a multi OODA loop body, each OODA loop must develop "right feel" for the others. The rider feels the horse, the horse feels the rider. For them to be martially effective, each must develop right feel.

But what does right feel consist of? I cannot answer this question, but we can observe some properties. One. Right feel is scale agnostic. It understands constitutive relationships. This feel is inseparable from its frame of reference. It is the qualia for an OODA loci. Finally, this feeling has three unique orientations. External-Internal (perception), Internal-External (intention), and Internal-Internal (proprioception). Both a commander and a soldier can collapse all their feeling to simply perception or intention, but from the cavalry corps perspective, part of what it 'feels' is proprioceptive feedback from the soldier's experience of the war theater. Part of what it 'intends' is derived from the commander's actions.

War and martial arts are useful for describing these phenomena because they paint action and intention in such stark outlines. In essence, they are a cartoon version of reality. There is us, there is them, there is our power, and there is theirs. But please note how this story transcends warfare and extends throughout our ancient and modern lives. The sword can just as easily be a pen; The horse can just as easily be a pet; the drone an autonomous car. What's important is the meditation on what it means to be me, given that I can change who I am purely based on what I can effectively feel.

Venkatesh Rao and the Protocol Institute introduced the term [New Nature](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/p/new-nature) to describe "Regimes of reality governed by technologically mediated laws that are nearly as inviolable, immutable, and persistent as those of nature itself." New Nature is here. It has been here for your entire life,
but it is quickly growing in fecundity. Mastery used to resemble that of the swordsman more so than the rider. Our tools didn't think back so much as they do now. So the question of how do you extend your embodiment through felt sense is all the more important.

Ethan Mollick and colleagues define two effective strategies for AI assisted workflows as becoming either a centaur or a cyborg:

> Centaur work has a clear line between person and machine, like the clear line between the human torso and horse body of the mythical centaur. Centaurs have a strategic division of labor, switching between AI and human tasks, allocating responsibilities based on the strengths and capabilities of each entity. ... Cyborgs blend machine and person, integrating the two deeply. Cyborgs don't just delegate tasks; they intertwine ... so that Cyborgs find themselves working in tandem with the AI.

([Centaurs and Cyborgs on the Jagged Frontier](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/centaurs-and-cyborgs-on-the-jagged)). 

A centaur's notional control is much more situated within the person, whereas the cyborg strategy spreads that out, diluting the person across the breadth of the AI assisted workflow. The centaur integrates, the cyborg assimilates. To me, there is clearly one right and one wrong strategy here. My identity is most uniquely "me" if I choose the path of the centaur. But just because it is the more principled approach does not necessarily make it the most effective. These strategies clearly have different properties in terms of their OODA loops and understanding those differences is the key to becoming both principled and effective.

Two cultural exemplars of what it means to be a centaur or cyborg are [Chiron](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Chiron-Greek-mythology) and [The Borg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borg).

Chiron is the half brother of Zeus, born from Chronos and a water nymph Philyra. Chiron was raised by Apollo because his mother rejected him when she saw his monstrous half-human half-beast appearance. Unlike most centaurs, which are described as bestial and savage, Chiron is described as supremely wise and is the teacher of many of the heroes of greek mythology: Heracles, Achilles and his father Peleus, as well as Jason of the Argonauts. Although immortal, he renounces his immortality and grants it to Prometheus. In recognition he was re-immortalized as the constellation Centaurus. 

Key traits emerge from the character of Chiron. He lives in the liminal space between savagery and deliberation. Although birthed from an act of trauma, he doesn't propagate that trauma. He directs his gifts towards the advancement of others. Paradoxically he rejects his immortality and thereby receives it. Chiron is principally an _integrator_, able to keep his parts distinct yet inseparable.

The Borg are much more contemporary, yet just as mythologically important. Where Chiron integrates, The Borg _assimilate_. In the Star Trek universe, these entities do not have a clear home or origin. They travel through the galaxy, overwhelming and absorbing cultures into their collective. They take the bodies and unique skills of their conquests and assimilate them into their body of drones. A borg drone is similar to an ant, it has no identity outside that of the collective. This collective is a Moloch. There is no real head. Killing one organ will not incapacitate the body. The drone's cyborg enhancements give them superhuman abilities that are shared by and indistinguishable from its collective siblings. Its skin is grey, and nanoprobes patrol its bloodstream, rewiring the host organism to the will of the collective at the deepest of levels.

Whereas any being can become the Borg, there can only be one Chiron. He is unique in his arc, his prowess, and his relationships. Centaurs preserve their individual identity and propagate heroes and their stories. We can't be Chiron, but we can be like him. If we don't learn from him, the unexamined path leads to being like the Borg.

It is this choice between integration and assimilation that characterizes the difference between a centaur and a cyborg. It's not what electromechanical implants a person has, it is their relation to them. A centaur takes capabilities and turns them into organs with roles and responsibilities. A cyborg takes capabilities and dissolves their identity into the mix. Like mixing colors you can start out with the same palette and paint either a brilliant sunset or a flat grey expanse. The difference isn't in the inputs, it is how you integrate and assimilate them.

New Nature is expanding our available palette, potentially leading to more ways to being uniquely you. Yet the danger with a more expansive palette is it is also easier to create indiscernable shades of grey. We can easily trick ourselves into thinking we have unique identities just because of the options we have available to style our appearance, neglecting what really counts, the style and substance of our principles and interior world.

New nature is burgeoning. We are embarking on a new Cambrian era. The new nature equivalent of multicellular life existed before this point, but it was slow and sluggish. We rode horses, we assembled ourselves into nations and corporations. But now it's becoming undeniable that my identity is not just my body, just as the identity of my eyeball is not just that of an eyeball. Nervous systems are growing. They are connecting different parts, forming bodies. The nature of the nervous system is a protocol for feeling. Out of feeling comes thought. The types of thought formed by that protocol can favor chiron-like or borg-like thoughts. Does _our_ new nature have a spine or an exoskeleton or give rise to a worm-like amorphous body?

# Part II: Sketching a Protocol For Proprioception in New Nature

## Purpose

As technology creators, we have a unique role in this Cambrian era of the New Nature. Body forms are assembling and testing themselves against each other. What structures survive and what go extinct has little to do with its effectiveness let alone its principles. Much of what survives is due to historical contingency. And yet, ideas can propagate. It's possible to design based on protocols in a manner that is both principled and effective. Because of contingency that doesn't guarantee its success but if the protocol is easily replicable it is much more likely. Here are the principles I believe determine whether we can embrace both our new nature and the spirit of Chiron.

## Principles

1. **Respect your Loci:** We have to consciously think of OODA loops and loci of control. We have to realize that "what it feels like to be you" is just one such loci. There are numerous loci within and extended from you.
2. **Organs not Implants:** We have to design our capabilities as organs, by which I mean they maintain a separateness with respect to their loci of control, but they are imbued with feeling. They feel the organism they are a part of and they provide feeling back to the organism.
3. **Can't be Evil by Construction:** We have to realize that the structure and design of organs is a collective effort. I didn't design my heart, I inherited it. And yet, my exercise, my diet, and my lifestyle make that heart uniquely mine. Getting this design process is an existential matter. As Venkatesh remarked, some systems have the property of Can't-be-evil, some just have the promise of don't-be-evil. The protocol for re-embodying ourselves must be **Capture-resistant by construction**.
4. **Chiron's Law:** We must respect that for proper loci of control to develop, we must lean into embodied training. Chiron doesn't fight Achilles fights, he teaches him the skills to fight his own battles. This is the difference between riding in a race car versus driving a race car. You can get to the same place, but the fear, exhilaration and crossover potential of the experience of the two is incomparable.

## Section 1: Building Organs

### Purpose
What does it mean to design a capability as an organ rather than a tool or implant?

### Principles
- **Organs, Not Implants**: Capabilities maintain separateness with respect to their loci of control, but are imbued with feeling. They feel the organism and provide feeling back.
- **Can't-Be-Evil by Construction**: Capture resistance is architectural, not moral. The protocol doesn't control the shape or function of organs, it enables them to integrate with each other via feeling.
- **Embodied principles**: Designing an organ means deciding: What does this entity observe? How does it orient? What can it decide? What can it act on? These are value questions.

### Design Elements
- **Boundary negotiation**: The body/environment boundary is not fixed. Like the immune system deciding self vs not-self. The protocol must support continuous negotiation. Organs can become cancerous (sub-OODA misaligned with collective) or be rejected (immune response to foreign body). Healthy integration = mutual feeling plus mutually respected constraints.
- **Pattern language for causality** (Christopher Alexander): Not imposed process but living patterns. Generative, not prescriptive. Each instantiation unique to context. Organs are patterns, not specifications. Each externalized organ adopted by a person both shapes and is shaped-by the person.
- **Act local, think global**: The design guild underwriting an organ is an epistemic collective — it explores and maintains the pattern. The person adapting the organ owns the pragmatic instantiation — it's theirs, configured to their context. But they hold an obligation to share back epistemically: redlines, adaptations, and trail-blazed paths feed the collective's understanding. Federated ownership: no single point of capture, pull-based adoption.
- **Guild architecture (the anti-monopoly problem)**: Collectives behind organs exist to epistemically explore a niche of embodied existence, not for instrumental utility. Medieval guilds warn of failure modes: gatekeeping, price-fixing, forced terms. When a guild optimizes for self-preservation rather than epistemic exploration, it becomes Seeing-Like-a-State at guild scale. Two architectural protections: **Opt-out** (exit without losing your own belief network or trail) and **Forking** (take patterns, instantiate new community with different orientation — epistemic speciation, not monopoly).
- **Procedures as protocol primitive**: Procedures declare the organ's world model — what it predicts, what it intends, how it acts in a way that adapts to human and machine understanding. Redlines signal surprise between organ and organism, enabling adaptation, or rejection based on the degree and type of surprise.

## Section 2: Designing Feedback

### Purpose
How does feeling flow between organs and organism? What makes proprioception work?

### Principles
- **Chiron's Law**: Teach the hero to fight, don't fight the hero's fights. Build capacity, don't outsource judgment.
- **Grace Before Feedback**: Can't train proprioception if you flinch from every signal. Grace is the anti-inflammatory that keeps the system from chronic rejection.
- **Invisibility Is a Feature**: Normal operation is invisible (Whitehead). Signals surface only when intervention is needed. Proprioception = felt sense INTO the invisible.

### Design Elements
- **Procedures = testable predictions about causality**: Making implicit world models explicit. "If I do X in context Y, Z will result." Not just your model — the organization's model, the technology's model. Procedures are how organs declare their world model to the organism.
- **Redlines = inflammation**: Where your model diverged from reality. Not failure — evidence. Organ → body: "I'm misaligned with what's actually happening." Body → organ: "Your orientation doesn't match collective values" (cancer detection). Healthy inflammation = temporary, resolves through adaptation. Chronic inflammation = systemic misalignment requiring structural change.
- **Grace as mechanism**: Without grace, shame blocks the learning loop. Shame = dissociating from your organs ("that wasn't really me"). Grace = owning the signal ("that was me, operating from a model that failed").
- **Graceful interfaces**: Time and training (sword becomes felt-body over years, not days). Routine vs interruption (you don't feel digestion working, you feel hunger/nausea/fullness). The nervous system works in three directions: internal-to-internal (organs coordinating), external-to-internal (sensing environment), internal-to-external (acting on environment).
- **Effortless effort (wu wei)**: The goal state. When feeling is so integrated that action flows without deliberation. This is what "inside the other's OODA loop" feels like in practice.
- **The progression** (honest about where we are): **Game tape** — retrospective observability, traceability, bi-directional markdown. **Mirror** (reaching for) — real-time proprioceptive feedback. **Dream / Primer** — generative training ground for building capacity. Procedures are the first step: a slow dream loop (predict → act → observe → redline → update). Guild brains get closer: collective training repertoire, distributions not prescriptions.

## Section 3: Evolution

### Purpose
How does this scale? How does it develop over a lifetime? Where does it lead?

### Principles
- **Scale-Free**: The same protocol serves the individual, the collective, and the nested (when I inhabit a role, I become a constituent cell). Same interface, different scale.
- **Weirdness Is the Signal**: Objective tools for perception increase weirdness; for control decrease it. Your local deviation IS the data.
- **No Walls to Mastery**: Smooth iterative deepening. No predetermined endpoint. Generative unfolding.

### Design Elements
- **Act local, think global**: Animals of the New Nature (companies, guilds, nations) are collective organisms with own OODA loops. Common protocol means we can inhabit them without losing our own felt sense. We can detect when the collective body is healthy vs cancerous. Seamless transition between independent and collective endeavors.
- **The commons defines body parts**: Capabilities, skills, knowledge structures available from federation. Pragmatic use: I use their training repertoire. Epistemic feedback: I feed back value through my bespoke viewpoint. Trail-blazing IS the contribution.
- **Generative skill tree**: NOT predefined skill trees. Start where you are → skill tree generatively unfolds → explore, adapt, adopt, strike out into niches that never existed → your trail becomes collective knowledge → others fold your path into the collective's potential paths.
- **Externalized world models as proprioceptive mirrors**: For the individual: belief networks = explicit map of your world models, the mirror to find YOUR grace (not conform to average). For the collective (organ genes): guild brains reveal the ORGANIZATION'S model. When 83% deviate at same step = org's model incompatible with reality. Guild brains show variance, not just mean.
- **Smooth iterative deepening**: A child learns to feel over years (limbs → tools → tools-as-body → lifetime). Start simple (model mapping), deepen anywhere (procedures, redlines, guild integration, environmental dialogue).
- **The Primer** (Stephenson): Not a commoditized service but a responsive training ground. Merges play and practice, suited to YOUR unique context. Generates curriculum from where you ARE. Future: personal context + computational capabilities = training ground responsive to individual weirdness. Federated dreaming: each person's training ground feeds collective while preserving sovereignty.
- **Kairos**: Building kairos = growing conditions where right moment emerges. Kairos lives in alignment between agents (your OODA loop + environment's OODA loop). Growth = principled search for kairos according to individual (weird) meaning. "Enlightenment is always accidental, but you can learn to be accident-prone."
