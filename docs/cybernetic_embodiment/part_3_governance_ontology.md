## Probe-gate definitions

Q-a, Q-c, and Q-g gate the bestiary's integration as a transmission kernel in Part V;
Q-b, Q-d, Q-e, Q-f are additional empirical bets. All are framed as falsifiable research
questions in **The Bets** below. The entries here are kept as quick-reference definitions; for the paradigm-grounded framing see **The Bets**, and for the canonical enumeration (invariants, experiments, kill conditions) the **unified register, Part V §9**.

- **Q-a --- separability.** Can the amendment-tolerance (see below for the definition) be empirically
  separated from ordinary internal policy drift? If not, the depth axis conflates two
  distinct phenomena and the profile is not a clean observable.
- **Q-b.** Two-regime boundary: where does "marginal disinterest + shared meta-egregore"
  shade into "full disinterest"? Is it a gradient (it probably is --- Section 15 marginal embodiment)?
- **Q-c --- minimality.** Does the bestiary stay small? The type space is subsets of
  `{o, s, C, p(o∣s)}` × depth --- bounded in principle, but types may still proliferate
  *within* that structure. If they do, the depth axis is mis-cut. Standing discipline:
  prefer collapsing a kind to adding a paragraph.
- **Q-d --- policy-profile invariance.** The shared-policy hypothesis predicts that exchange
  dynamics are driven by *profile* (co-held depth of `{o, s, C, p(o∣s)}`), not by surface
  mechanism. A clean test: identify two exchange classes --- one trust-based, one formally
  mediated --- where the underlying shared policy profile can be inferred to be similar, and
  show that their exchange dynamics converge. Convergence confirms that profile is the
  typology key; divergence means mechanism is doing more work than the hypothesis allows.
- **Q-e.** Authority/head vs field/standard: both are "egregores," but one issues verdicts.
  Does the bestiary cut on faced/faceless (synthesis Section 1 head/field) cleanly, or do hybrids
  dominate in practice?
- **Q-f (bootstrap).** Does the referent *install* the shared sign or *presuppose* it?
  Likely dynamic --- repeated bargaining **thickens** the intersection (you come to care about
  money by transacting in it) --- but some seed care is needed to start. "Installs" holds
  developmentally; the bootstrap is unmodeled, and it gates whether a thick co-constitutive
  layer can be *grown* or only inherited.
- **Q-g --- computability and predictivity.** Is the co-held subset of
  `{o, s, C, p(o∣s)}` computable from compiled policies (per-component policy overlap
  between each party and their shared referent)? And does depth of co-holding measurably
  predict amendment-tolerance --- and, by extension, propagation behavior (reverberation
  dynamics, topology priors)? This is the bestiary's central empirical bet; the catalog
  names *forms*, but their value as a transmission kernel depends on this holding.

**amendment tolerance** = the maximum amendment depth a party consistently accepts from a partner before the relationship changes character (renegotiation, defection, decay). That's the observable Q-g actually needs, and it's nowhere stated explicitly.

## The relational taxonomy (3-axis reconstruction)

> **SCAFFOLD.** This section supersedes the forces and sovereigns tables in the relocated
> Part II material below. The old tables are kept for prose reference; this framework is
> the ontological basis for integration. Under the as-if / materialist stance, the
> forces / sovereigns binary dissolves into a continuous 3-axis space.

### Primary axes

**1. Dynamic model** --- can you predictively model the thing’s trajectory well enough to
use it as an effector?

**2. Normative model** --- does the thing have preferences you can model (as-if)? The
as-if stance: imputing normativity as a modeling affordance, not a metaphysical claim.

**3. Perceptual depth** --- your stance: *numb* (no engagement) / *sensing only* (receives
signal, cannot project intention through it) / *sensing + effecting* (full bidirectional
engagement).

**Derived axes:**

- **Sign** --- `dC_you / dC_other` in the shared environment. A marginal, contextual
  computation: does the other’s normative motion move your `C` toward or away from your
  preferences? Opens wherever a normative model is present. The same relation can be
  positive on one referent, negative on another.
- **Governed / ungoverned** --- whether you use the thing as an **effector** (project
  intention through it). Requires a dynamic model. You can *contain* chaos (firewall,
  insulation) but not govern it (no reliable trajectory-projection without dynamic model).

### The four cells

| | **No normative model** | **Yes normative model** |
|---|---|---|
| **No dynamic model** | **A --- Chaos** | **B --- Egregore family** |
| **Yes dynamic model** | **C --- Mechanism family** | **D --- Sovereign family** |

#### Cell A --- Chaos

No reliable handle for prediction or normativity.

- **Numb**: pure chaos (weather, crowd, random process)
- **Sensing only --- the sublime**: chaos acting as a *sensing instrument that reads your
  own `C` landscape*. You cannot feel *into* it (no normative structure to model); the
  surprise reverberates against your own preferences and changes your normative bandpass.
  The feeling is reflective, not external --- a lens on your own normativity. Always
  ungoverned (effecting through chaos has no reliable trajectory).
- Sensing + effecting: incoherent without dynamic model.

#### Cell B --- Egregore family

Normative pull without a tractable dynamic model (market, culture, shared standard ---
you cannot model its mechanics, but you can model its preferences).

- **Numb**: field-egregore (price, pheromone, cultural norm --- normative pull you
  coordinate with but don’t feel into)
- **Normative signal accessible** (e.g., annealing gradient `dΔ/dt` is observable):
  **functionally sovereign**. Under the materialist / as-if stance, a faced egregore and
  a sovereign will are the same object --- both are as-if projections onto a substrate,
  distinguished only by the richness and accessibility of the normative signal.
- **Sign**: `dC_you / dC_other` via the egregore’s normative gradient.
- **Wound**: `dΔ/dt > 0` from your interaction = distributed normative wound. *“No wound
  lands on a single someone”* --- the wound is real and normatively significant; what is
  absent is a centralized experiencer, not the wound itself. Accountability is distributed,
  not absent.

#### Cell C --- Mechanism family

Predictable trajectory; no preferences to model.

- **Numb**: pure mechanism (RNG, three-body problem, physical force --- mathematical
  model available, no as-if normative stance warranted)
- **Sensing only**: mechanism as passive sensing instrument (gauge, detector)
- **Sensing + effecting**: **tool / extension** --- the sword, the lever. Governed: you
  project intention through its dynamics. The tool / extension distinction maps onto
  perceptual depth.

#### Cell D --- Sovereign family

All of the original eight-ways sovereign taxonomy (see relocated table below). The sign
axis opens contextually as a marginal computation.

- Numb + ungoverned: **wild** / adversary
- Numb + governed: **coerced** (projecting intention while ignoring their normativity)
- Sensing + effecting + sign `+`: **partner, beloved** --- their normative motion moves your
  `C` in the same direction
- Sensing + effecting + sign `−`: **enemy, tormented** --- their normative motion moves your
  `C` in the opposite direction
- **Wound**: `C` violation → free energy spike, centralized in that sovereign’s experience

### Five moves from the old to the new

1. **“Forces / sovereigns” → dynamic × normative.** No hard ontological threshold --- a
   continuous spectrum. The binary was a pedagogical simplification.
2. **Egregore can feel a wound.** `dΔ/dt > 0` = distributed normative wound. The old
   claim tracked topology (distributed vs. centralized), not presence / absence of wound.
3. **Faced egregore = sovereign** (materialist view). Access to the normative signal
   collapses the distinction. Both are as-if projections; the difference is signal
   richness and accessibility.
4. **Sublime = Cell A + sensing only; feeling is reflective.** The sublime is not a
   relation *to* the chaos --- chaos acts as a mirror for your own `C` landscape, not a
   window onto an external normative structure.
5. **Sign is contextual and marginal.** `dC_you / dC_other` in the shared environment.
   Fixed-sign labels (always enemy, always partner) are approximations of the marginal
   default. Same relation, different referent = different sign.

### Sovereign prior consequence

The prior (default to probing for normativity) applies across **all four cells** --- any
could have a normative model you are missing. Cost of wrong denial: you miss the normative
signal and steer open-loop against something that was feeling you back.

### The cost is a sign (the moral sign apparatus)

> *(← Part II, "The cost is a sign". Dangling refs to fix: "its scale floor, below" now
> points at Part II's *Scale* honesty; "the last section" references from Part II's "Right is
> fit" now point here.)*

If feeling were just a superb model of the other, the coldest predators would be the
most loving. They are not. A hunter holds an exquisite model of its prey and is not
wounded by it; the con artist, the interrogator, the propagandist model us *best*,
because modeling-without-stakes is exactly what predation rewards. High-resolution
modeling of another's inner life correlates, if anything, with exploitation. So whatever feeling
costs, the cost cannot live in the modelling itself.

It lives in **whose preferences yours answer to.** The exploiter keeps the other
wholly in the column of *what it will do* --- a matter of prediction, instrumental and
costless to revise. Feeling is letting the other's wants start to count in your *own*
tally of what makes a good or bad outcome --- so that a state of the world registers as
*your* win or *your* loss by the *other's* lights. And that entry has a **sign**,
revealed by what you would pay:

- **With (+)** --- you would spend to see the other's preferred states satisfied. Care.
- **Numb (0)** --- you weight its preferences at nothing. The hunter wants food, not the
  deer's suffering, and takes a costless kill gladly. This is the dashboard, however
  sharp its model.
- **Against (−)** --- you would pay to thwart it *as an end*: spite, harm pursued past
  the point it serves you. Animosity (Hamilton's rare, expensive vice).

Both care and animosity have pragmatic *cost*; only the numb dashboard is free. That is what
"feeling has to cut" means, made exact. To put a positive sign on another's principles is to
enlarge the set of world-states that count as *your* loss to include ones you do not
control --- so when the other suffers, something *you* count as a loss has happened, and
because the other is sovereign and sometimes beyond your reach, it is a surprise you
often cannot discharge by any act of your own. That undischargeable surprise, lodged at
the level of what you care about, *is* the wound; it has the same structure as grief. It
does not breach the locality of will: you never import the other's preferences; you add a
*local, sovereign* entry to your own that merely *refers to* their flourishing.

This sign is exactly what tells a partner from a captive: same orders, same model, opposite sign on the other's principles in your own ledger. But do not mistake it for mere *mutual benefit*. Yoked fitness is cheap and ancient --- a mitochondrion and its host have had coupled fitnesses for a billion years and feel each other not at all, coordinating by chemistry alone. Mutualism collects the winnings of a shared interest for free. *Care* pays an ante to sit at the table, and the ante is **vulnerability** --- non-refundable, paid in advance: to put a positive sign on another's principles simply *is* to stand exposed to a loss you cannot discharge. This vulnerability enables the partner and you to grow into mutual benefit, but risks your sovereign capture or coercion as well.

One asymmetry underwrites all of this. You cannot put a sign on a principle you have not modeled: the model is the *substrate*, the sign is painted on it. Care presupposes the dashboard; the dashboard presupposes no care, and can stand alone --- which is just to say the exploiter is feeling's substrate without its sign. So feeling-into costs *twice over* --- once in the compute to raise the model at all (its scale floor, below), once in the stake of the sign --- and the two costs are not the same cost. A protocol can build the substrate and drive the first cost toward nothing; the second it can never pay for you. That the same model serves the lover and the con artist is not a flaw in the model --- it is what it means for the sign to be sovereign.

### The sovereign relations (the "eight ways", sovereign half)

> *(← Part II, "Eight ways to hold another", the sovereign square. Part II keeps the
> three-axis structure, the perception threshold, and the *forces* table; this is the
> half that opens above the threshold, where the sign axis lives. The closing
> portfolio/gardener paragraph concludes the *whole* relational taxonomy --- forces + these ---
> so on integration it may need a bridging sentence back to Part II's forces table.)*

**Sovereigns** --- perception above the threshold, where the sign axis opens. Control
now splits: *coerce* (override, unfelt) or *lead* (through the bond). And "felt,"
which always quietly meant *moved* and assumed *moved meant for*, gains a valence ---
the enemy is fiercely felt, just felt *against*. The live face is intention × sign:

| | **against (−)** would harm | **numb (0)** instrumental | **with (+)** would help |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ungoverned** | the enemy *(would eradicate)* | the wild / adversary | the beloved *(would die for)* |
| **governed** | the tormented *(victim)* | the coerced *(conscript)* | the partner *(led through care)* |

Read it by dark-twin pairs --- same control, opposite sign. **Beloved ↔ enemy**:
ungoverned and fully felt, die-for against would-kill. **Partner ↔ tormented**:
intentional care vs intentional cruelty. **Wild ↔
coerced**: the numb middle. The sharp claim is that partner, coerced, and tormented
are three points on *one* axis under identical control --- care, indifference, cruelty.
The tyrant is not the opposite of the lover; the *indifferent* master is the
midpoint, and the tyrant is the lover's mirror across zero. (The negative pole is
sovereign-only: you cannot hate what has no preferences. To hate a force you must
first animate it --- the cruel sea, the disease you "fight." Hatred runs the sovereign
prior; animosity is a *form* of attribution, not its absence.)

**The coerced** is a captured will, directed
unfelt --- the conscript, the farmed aphid, the parasitized host: *retained* but
overridden, captured *by* you, not arrayed *against* you. This is the domination cell,
wrong precisely where a tack existed and you severed feeling to override instead of
sailed through. And it is dynamical: coerce a will long enough and you grind it out --- *then* you
have a true **drone**, will-less rigging, the Borg's grey instrument of Part I, which
belongs *below* the line with tool and extension, not in the sovereign square at all.

**The adversary** shares the **wild's** cell --- an unfeeling sovereign you do not govern
--- read at opposed alignment. You can model it fully (predict-to-defend) but cannot
feel *into* it, and cannot simply set its next state; you contest it. Wild and
adversary differ only in *whether a tack exists*: the wild's is unknown (a tack may
exist, unfound --- what the sovereign prior reads hopefully), the adversary's is opposed.
Keep "no tack exists" **provisional** --- almost always it is "no tack *found yet*."
Premature "adversary" is self-fulfilling: coerce a misread wild and its adversarial
prior makes the opposition real, manufacturing the enemy you assumed. Most enmities
have a tack somewhere --- even the USA and USSR kept a scientific channel open across the
worst of it --- and often a relation splits cleanly: adversarial on one referent,
coordinating on another.

One move along the **intention** axis needs care: imposing direction does not shrink a
will, it sets the **scope** it ranges over --- what it attends to, where its concern
ends. The brain sets the liver's scope and the liver bears no grievance; the
constraint is legitimate and load-bearing *when felt* (the brain holds the liver as an
extension --- unilateral feeling, not partnership). Its two pathologies are both severed
feeling: a scope-setter gone numb makes the coerced; a scoped part gone numb to its
setter makes **cancer**, a sub-loop reclaiming scope the body can no longer feel. The
ethic is never in the constraint, only in whether feeling stays live across it.

So right relation is not the one cell that excludes the seven. A living body runs a
**portfolio** --- wielding tools, harnessing forces, reading the sublime, contesting
adversaries, and feeling its sovereigns rather than using them --- and moves each thing
between corners as its nature changes. The work is the **gardener's**: holding each
thing in the relation that fits it, and growing the conditions under which it might
become the relation it could. Chiron is not a corner of this field. Chiron is its
gardener.

### Generativity (selection is death)

> *(← Part II, "Head, field, or feeling", the *Generativity* honesty. The *Scale* honesty
> travels here too --- see the *Head, field, or feeling* block below; this one is Project-A ---
> evolution, play, legibility-vs-fitness.)*

*Generativity.* The surprise that flows between organs is not error to be averaged
toward a mean; it is *variation*. But variation is only half of evolution. A body
that grinds every deviation back to the mean ossifies; a body that folds in every
deviation dissolves toward noise. A generative body keeps a deviation alive long
enough to be *tested* --- and where the test fails, lets it die. Selection is death,
and the two are one motion; the cost is real and no optimizer waives it. Feeling
steers the testing --- *play* is testing by feeling rather than by consequence, and fun
is not vague: it is prediction error falling toward a living floor (Schmidhuber's
*fun*, Oudeyer and Kaplan's *learning progress*). But play can lie. Feeling selects
for what *pays to model* --- legibility --- and legibility is not fitness. A curiosity
drive left alone fixates on the most learnable thing, not the most adaptive; that way
lie peacock tails and pugs that cannot breathe. So feeling-selection is real but not
*sufficient* selection: play must stay tethered to the slow, costly test where the
world, not the body's delight, casts the deciding vote. Feeling proposes; consequence
disposes. (This is the antibody to any reading of feeling as pure efficiency: it
selects for the legible, which is not the fit.)

### Head, field, or feeling (the body-forms --- the step from dyad to body)

> *(← Part II, "Head, field, or feeling" --- the whole section. This sentence --- "Step back from
> the dyad to the body it composes" --- *is* the Part II → Part III boundary: Part II is feeling
> between a self and one other (or a self reflecting on itself); this is what *whole* many such
> relations compose. Its governance pieces (the constitution as field-encoded federation; the
> *Reachability* honesty) are in Part VI; the *Generativity* honesty is the sibling block
> above; the *Scale* honesty travels here, below. Integration notes: the read-off's
> "federation frozen into law" refers to the constitution material now in Part VI; Part VII
> will formalize the read-off as a body-form detection device.)*

Step back from the dyad to the body it composes. A body is the **same space, read as
statistics over its dyads**: where the *intention* axis concentrates, and what the
*perception* axis points at across its edges. Three sources of coherence answer --- but
they are not three of a kind, and treating them as peers is the mistake this section
undoes.

- A **head** --- one locus whose will propagates to the rest (intention concentrated).
  Felt downward, the parts are subordinate layers and the whole is an *organism*;
  commanded numb, they are rigging and the whole is a *hierarchy of drones*. A head
  scales by construction: one will, propagated.
- A **field** --- an external medium every part minimizes against, with no head and no
  mutual modeling (perception pointed at a shared referent). This is the *egregore*:
  the ant colony reading pheromone, the market reading price, the culture reading
  story. None has a head; no part feels another; yet they cohere, persist, and
  routinely out-muscle the deliberate bodies around them. What the egregore lacks is
  not power but *interiority* --- no loss lands as a wound, so there is no one to hold to
  account. Part I's ghosts and demons: potent, ungraspable, unaccountable, stable for
  centuries. A field, too, scales by construction: one medium, read by all.
- **Feeling** --- the parts model *each other* (perception pointed at each other, the
  sign live), holding distributed will with no head and no medium. Here the symmetry
  breaks: feeling is not a third *architecture* beside the other two but a property of
  a **dyad**. What it composes --- the **felt federation** --- mirrors the egregore in the
  one respect that matters: its parts bleed for one another, so harm lands somewhere
  inside it and the buck can stop. But where a head and a field scale by construction,
  feeling does not scale on its own at all. Why, and what it borrows to go large
  anyway, is the rest of this section.

A field and a feeling look alike --- both headless, both distributed --- and the
difference is the whole game. A field is *objective*: the mark is the same to
everyone, carries no provenance, and is read without modeling anyone. Feeling is
*subjective*: a redline is inert until a receiver runs it through its own model of who
sent it, so the coordination lives not in any shared medium but in the private,
divergent models the parts hold of one another. A field coordinates strangers;
feeling can only pass between a someone and a someone.

So feeling builds only dyads, and two costs keep a body of them small. The first is
the **cut**: every partner bond is a non-refundable ante of vulnerability --- a standing
loss-channel you cannot discharge --- so *n* partners means *n* wounds, climbing in a
line. The second compounds: partners' normativities do not politely agree, so
satisfying one redline can violate another, making a many-bond body a **frustrated
network** --- a rugged landscape of shallow basins, no global rest, stability always
local and provisional. A felt federation is therefore not a *form you build* but an
*equilibrium you are lucky to occupy* --- rare, small, metastable for the very reason it
is unseizable. So nature, reaching for scale, reaches almost always for a field or a
head.

Read which form a body has fallen into from the two aggregate questions, no
declaration required --- they are the dyadic axes summed: *where does the intention axis
concentrate*, and *what does each part's perception point at*? A dominant locus is a
**head** --- organism if its parts are felt downward, drone-hierarchy if merely
commanded. No dominant locus is **headless**, sorted by the second question: perception
on a shared external referent is an **egregore**; on each other's policies, a **felt
federation** --- though a federation frozen into law now minimizes against *that* artifact
and reads egregore by this crude test, so what tells them apart is not the medium but
whether a loss still lands on a someone inside. A headless body that points perception
at *neither* has no coordination source at all: persistence pressure makes it
crystallize a head, but a head that withholds feeling steers blind and slumps back
toward diffuse. That is not a fourth form but a **trajectory** --- an orbit between
drone-hierarchy and fieldless egregore, holding neither corner.

*Scale.* Feeling is not free, and not everywhere. Coordination is **step-wise ordered** --- with three phase changes.

At the base, **pure field and gain**: loops react to gradients in a shared medium --- no persistent preferred state, no setpoint, no wound. Purely reactive: follows the current gradient without defending anything. The right register for chemistry, pheromone plumes, voltage concentration without homeostatic targets.

Above it, the **homeostatic threshold** --- the minimum-ante layer: a loop maintains one or more stable preferred states---attractors it returns to after perturbation. Having a setpoint *is* having something to defend and something that can be wounded; this is the epistemic transition from reactive to normative. The formal protocol’s total logic (`o`, `s`, `C`, `p(o∣s)`) is one design choice at this layer --- more structured, diffable, and publishable --- but the layer itself is defined by homeostasis, not by whether that logic is implemented. Bioelectric morphogenesis lives here: a cell reads the tissue’s gap-junction voltage network and defends the target morphology against perturbation --- goal-carrying, directional, substrate-encoded --- without implementing explicit logic or modeling its neighbors as having wills. Sub-logics can coordinate at this layer on lower memory and compute footprints; they are readable by the full protocol as parties with normativity, whether or not they run the protocol themselves.

Feeling has its own, higher floor: to *feel* another you must model its normativity in your own head, which is strictly more than modeling yourself. Below that floor, *and that is the right register there, not a failure*: the cells of a tissue do not feel one another. A real body contains elements of all three computational phases --- feeling, minimum-ante coordination, and pure field and gain. Most of life's great partnerships run on that middle band and are no poorer for it: the endosymbioses, the lichens, the microbiome are mutualisms run on chemistry and setpoint, fitnesses yoked without a flicker of feeling --- which is why a felt federation's distinctive claim is not that its parts stay undissolved (cheap, and common) but that they stay undissolved *and feel one another* (rare).

## The Bets

**Bet 2 --- network identification.**

Given a network of players, each of which can set up contracts with other players through the network, and each of which have different policies guiding the type of contracts they value. Each contract is a commitment that on the onset the individuals do not have to be able to fulfil. A fulfilled contract has a different value to the receiver than what the sender spends to fulfill it. Players iterate play by proposing or approving new contracts, modifications to existing contracts, or cancelling contracts or fulfilling them.

If the network is observable, global network measurement optima could be pre-computed on this set up, and the state of iterated play compared to that.

We hypothesize that the thickness of the policy shared between a set of players is highly correlated with the dynamics of the 'reverberations' on any of these types of change as well as the likelihood of finding a global optima. The bestiary serves to catalog the kernels that efficiently describe these reverberations.

This measure can be compared to alternatives, such as a measure of how 'reliable' a contract partner is perceived to be.

### Component bets and gating conditions

Seven empirical questions condition Bet 2. Three are **Q-gates** (Q-a, Q-c, Q-g) --- they
must have defensible answers before the co-held policy profile is used as a transmission
kernel in Bet 2 or Part V Section 5. The remaining four are **open empirical bets** the
framework makes that do not block execution.

#### Gating bets (Q-a, Q-c, Q-g)

**Q-g --- computability and predictivity.** Is the co-held subset of `{o, s, C, p(o∣s)}`
computable from compiled policies (per-component overlap between each party and their shared
referent)? And does the depth of co-holding measurably predict amendment-tolerance --- and,
by extension, propagation behavior (reverberation dynamics, topology priors)? This is the
bestiary's central empirical bet; the catalog names *forms*, but their value as a transmission
kernel depends on this holding.

**Q-c --- minimality.** Does the bestiary stay small? The type space is subsets of
`{o, s, C, p(o∣s)}` × depth --- bounded in principle, but types may proliferate *within*
that structure. If they do, the depth axis is mis-cut. Standing discipline: prefer collapsing
a kind to adding an entry.

**Q-a --- separability.** Can the amendment-depth signature (how far a party revises its
own policy `C` to complete a transaction) be empirically separated from ordinary internal
policy drift? If not, the depth axis conflates two distinct phenomena and the profile is not
a clean observable.

#### Additional empirical bets (Q-b, Q-d, Q-e, Q-f)

**Q-b --- disinterest is a gradient, not a threshold.** Where does marginally-engaged exchange
shade into full disinterest? Bet: the transition is a **continuous gradient** in exchange
dynamics (no phase transition), varying smoothly with co-held profile depth. A sharp threshold
would appear as a discontinuity in amendment-depth tolerance; smooth degradation would support
the gradient. The marginal-embodiment reading predicts the gradient.

**Q-d --- policy-profile invariance.** The shared-policy hypothesis predicts that exchange
dynamics are driven by *profile* (co-held depth of `{o, s, C, p(o∣s)}`), not by surface
mechanism. Test: identify two exchange classes --- one trust-based, one formally mediated ---
where the underlying shared policy profile can be inferred to be similar, and show that their
exchange dynamics converge. Convergence confirms that profile is the typology key; divergence
means mechanism is doing more work than the hypothesis allows.

**Q-e --- C-sign predicts faced vs. faceless.** The sign of the co-held `C` --- **positive
meet** (states to pursue together) vs **negative envelope** (states to avoid) --- predicts
whether an egregore centralizes decision authority (faced, verdict-issuing) or distributes it
(faceless, field-like). A positive-meet `C` pulls members toward convergence and is head-prone;
a negative-envelope `C` forbids only and leaves interiors free, remaining field-like. Bet: in
a dataset of egregores (institutions, markets, protocols), the positive-meet/negative-envelope
distinction in co-held `C` reliably predicts faced vs. faceless topology. Hybrids --- some `C`
positive-meet, some negative-envelope --- predict partial centralization, a testable
intermediate signature.

  *Under the consolidated ontology (3-axis taxonomy):* faced / faceless is the equilibrium
  topology of Cell B (egregore family) determined by C-sign. When the annealing signal
  (`dΔ/dt`) is accessible to inhabitants, a positive-meet egregore becomes functionally
  sovereign (Cell D) --- the faced / faceless distinction is therefore a test of *normative
  signal accessibility*, not merely topology. Three independently-resolvable sub-tests:
  (a) does C-sign predict topology at equilibrium? (b) does C-sign predict annealing type
  (attractor / floor)? (c) when `dΔ/dt` is accessible, does coordination improve relative
  to when it is not?

**Q-f --- profile thickness is grown, not inherited.** Does the shared referent *install*
co-held `C` through repeated transactions, or must some threshold co-holding *presuppose* the
exchange? Bet (install): in a longitudinal dataset of exchange relationships, profile thickness
(co-held `C` breadth, amendment-depth tolerance) increases over the history of the
relationship. Stable co-holding from first contact would support the presuppose hypothesis
instead.
